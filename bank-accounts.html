Before Using a Financial Service

A few important points to keep in mind.

Security & Verification

Financial services may have identity, verification, eligibility and regional requirements. Always use official channels when opening or managing a financial account.

Use accurate personal or business information.
Review the provider's current terms.
Never share passwords or security codes.

Availability, features, fees and eligibility can vary by provider and region. Please verify the latest information directly with the relevant financial service provider.

Check current availability.
